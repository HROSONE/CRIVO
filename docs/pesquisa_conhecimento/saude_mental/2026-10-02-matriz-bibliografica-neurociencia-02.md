# Quantitative discipline added

## Diagnostic usefulness
For any candidate biomarker:
`pretest prevalence → sensitivity/specificity → LR+/LR- → post-test probability → calibration → decision consequence`.

Example conceptual:
Even 90% sensitivity and 90% specificity can produce many false positives when target prevalence is very low. Therefore AUC without intended-use population is insufficient.

## Effect size versus classification
A reproducible standardized mean difference of 0.2 can be scientifically real while distributions overlap heavily. Group discovery and individual diagnosis answer different questions.

## Biomarker versus pathology
A biomarker may measure pathology while clinical syndrome depends on location, burden, reserve, co-pathology and time.

## Genetic penetrance
`P(phenotype|variant)` must not be confused with `P(variant|phenotype)`.
Penetrance is not fixed independent of ascertainment/age/environment.
